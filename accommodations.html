Other nearby towns include Nominingue, Macaza, and Ste Véronique. There is also a variety of camping options nearby.

Further away, about 1.5 hours, is Tremblant, a famous ski town. It has a LOT of accommodations, generally more upscale, and a variety of restaurants, activities, and amenities.
